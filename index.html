FAQ

Questions students ask

How do I get the password?

MentorHub is for enrolled students. Ask your teacher for the class password. You only need to enter it once on each device.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, automatically, in the browser you study on. To move to another phone or computer, use Progress → Download backup and then Restore on the new device.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It works on any modern phone, tablet or computer. Tip: use your browser's "Add to Home screen" to open it like an app.

Does it follow my university syllabus?

Yes. The Statistics course follows the official Osmania University first-year syllabus, and every syllabus item links to the lessons that teach it.

Are the model papers real exam papers?

No. They are practice papers built in the official OU layout. Questions from a verified previous OU paper are clearly labelled; everything else is labelled as practice.
